Outreach and engagement

Brain-Drone Race

Lawrence Technological University, in collaboration with The University of Alabama

Coordinated and delivered LTU’s first Brain-Drone Race, a public event in which participants fly drones using brain-computer interfaces, bringing together neuroscience, robotics, and AI. The initiative was later presented to the department’s Industrial Advisory Board as a model for interdisciplinary, industry-relevant education.

Education and Workforce Training Structure, Alabama IMaDE Center

The University of Alabama

Helped build the instructional and workforce-development infrastructure for the B.S. in Manufacturing Systems Engineering, aligning the curriculum with automation, robotics, PLCs, digital twins, and smart manufacturing.

  • Developed 15 modular courses, lab manuals, and digital-twin-enabled instructional materials.
  • Implemented lab workflows connecting automation hardware with virtual manufacturing environments.
  • Supported partnerships with Mercedes-Benz U.S. International, Rockwell Automation, EBZ SysTec, Intelitek, Learning Labs, and TransfrVR.

Smart Manufacturing Systems Teacher Training Workshop

Alabama IMaDE Center, two-day workshop

Organized and delivered a two-day workshop introducing educators to robotics, PLCs, smart sensors, VR, and automated systems through lectures, lab tours, and hands-on sessions, with Intelitek, Mercedes-Benz U.S. International, Alabama Robotics Technology Park, TransfrVR, and the Alabama Community College System.
