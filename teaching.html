Teaching

My teaching focuses on control, robotics, and intelligent systems, with an emphasis on hands-on learning, project-based assignments, and connecting theory to real robotic systems.

Teaching interests

Control Systems, Mechatronics, Robotics, Human-Robot Interaction, Machine Learning, Brain-Computer Interfaces

Teaching approach

I aim to create an inclusive, supportive classroom where students build both conceptual understanding and practical skills. I use applied examples, structured milestones, and team projects to help students develop confidence in designing, implementing, and evaluating robotic systems.

Recognized with the Outstanding Teaching Award, Department of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Alabama (2022).
